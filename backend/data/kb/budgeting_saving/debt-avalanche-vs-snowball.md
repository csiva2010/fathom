# Paying Off Debt: Avalanche vs Snowball

When you have several debts, the order in which you pay them off matters. Two popular strategies are the avalanche and the snowball. Both start the same way: make the minimum payment on every debt, then put every extra dollar toward one target debt.

## Debt avalanche

Target the debt with the highest interest rate first. When it is paid off, move to the next-highest rate. Mathematically, the avalanche minimizes the total interest you pay and usually gets you debt-free fastest.

## Debt snowball

Target the debt with the smallest balance first, regardless of rate. When it is gone, roll its payment into the next-smallest balance. The quick early wins can build motivation, which research suggests helps some people stick with the plan.

## Which works better?

The avalanche saves the most money; the snowball may be easier to sustain. The best choice is the one you will actually follow. Some people use a hybrid: knock out one tiny balance for momentum, then switch to the avalanche.

## Other tools

- **Balance transfer cards** with a 0% introductory rate can cut interest, but usually charge a transfer fee and a high rate after the promotion ends.
- **Debt consolidation loans** can combine debts at a lower rate, if your credit qualifies.
- **Nonprofit credit counseling** agencies can help set up a debt management plan.

## Avoid adding new debt

Paying down balances works only if new borrowing stops. A small emergency fund helps prevent unexpected expenses from going back onto credit cards.
